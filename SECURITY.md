# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue. Use GitHub's private reporting
([Security > Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/juanpacj-wq/juancespedes.dev/security/advisories/new))
or write to juanpablocespedesj@gmail.com with the subject `SECURITY`.

You can expect an acknowledgement within 3 business days and a fix or mitigation plan within
14 days for confirmed issues.

## Scope

The production site at `juancespedes.dev`, its API endpoints under `/api/*`, and this repository's
CI configuration. Third-party services (Cloudflare, GitHub, email and AI providers) are out of scope.
