problem: Operations needed to see, on one screen, the actual output of four generating units against what the market operator dispatched, and the existing readings depended on a centralized system that was a single point of failure.
constraints:
  - Readings every 2 seconds without losing the energy integration if the service restarts.
  - Two plants with different sign conventions depending on the metering boundary.
  - A failed meter must not pollute totals with false zeros.
decisions:
  - title: Read the meters directly over Modbus TCP
    rationale: Removes the single point of failure and the automated browser from the deployment, and makes each read's latency measurable.
    alternatives:
      - Keep scraping the centralized system's web interface
  - title: Null means no reading, never zero
    rationale: Telling missing data apart from a zero value makes a failed meter detectable instead of reporting zero output.
    alternatives:
      - Fill missing readings with zero
  - title: Statistical validation before trusting the backup meters
    rationale: A one-hour parallel capture with bias, p95 and integrated-energy criteria showed the backup meters are interchangeable.
    alternatives:
      - Assume the backup meters read the same
lessons:
  - Documenting the sign convention with parallel readings on migration day prevented weeks of arguments about numbers.
